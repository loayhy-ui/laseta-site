Job Application Guide • Saudi Arabia

How to tailor your CV to one real vacancy without making anything up.

The fastest way to make a CV more relevant is not to redesign everything. It is to compare the vacancy with your real background and make the strongest overlap easy to see.

The five-step vacancy map

01Extract
List responsibilities, skills, tools and qualifications.
02Evidence
Match each requirement to something you have actually done.
03Rewrite
Bring the most relevant evidence forward in accurate language.
04Verify
Check dates, titles, facts and terminology for consistency.

What to change

  • Target job title where accurate.
  • Summary so it reflects the specific role.
  • Order and wording of relevant experience bullets.
  • Skills list so genuine role-relevant skills are visible.
  • Application or cover message to explain fit briefly.

What not to change

  • Do not change employment dates to look stronger.
  • Do not add software you have never used.
  • Do not claim revenue, team size or achievements you cannot prove.
  • Do not copy every keyword from the vacancy.
  • Do not create a qualification simply because the employer asks for it.

Common questions

Should every application have a different CV?

You do not need to rebuild the entire document. The core history can stay stable while the summary, relevant bullets and skills emphasis change to match the genuine evidence most relevant to the role.

What if I do not meet every requirement?

Do not hide the gap with invented information. Show the closest real evidence and let the employer decide whether the gap is acceptable.

Should I use an automated ATS score?

It can be a diagnostic signal, but a score is not a hiring decision. Relevance, truthfulness, readability and human judgment still matter.
