Riyadh Household Guide

Compare home-service quotes by scope first, price second.

Two Riyadh providers can quote very different totals because the work included is different. Normalize the scope before deciding which price is actually better.

Normalize these fields

  • Property size and service type.
  • Labour hours or crew size.
  • Equipment and materials included.
  • Travel, call-out or inspection charges.
  • Add-ons and exclusions.
  • Earliest available service date.

Current Riyadh examples

Published providers show how much scope changes price. CleanDari currently publishes hourly cleaning from SAR 119, deep cleaning from SAR 549, move-in/move-out cleaning from SAR 649 and AC cleaning from SAR 79 per unit. Those are provider prices, not universal market rates.

See CleanDari published pricing

The cheapest quote is not automatically the lowest total cost.

A lower quote can exclude supplies, windows, appliances, transportation or other work that another provider included. Put every quote into the same comparison table before deciding.

Questions to ask before booking

Is the quote fixed or subject to inspection?

Ask what conditions can change the final price and who confirms the final amount.

What exactly is excluded?

Exclusions often explain most of the difference between two prices.

Who is responsible for the service quality?

The selected provider performs the underlying service. A comparison service can help organize the decision but does not replace the provider's responsibility for its work.
