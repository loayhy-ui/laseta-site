Micro-Business Guide • Saudi Arabia

WhatsApp selling works better when the customer can understand the offer quickly.

The free WhatsApp Business app already gives small sellers useful tools such as a business profile, catalogue, quick replies and labels. The commercial problem is usually not the app itself; it is unclear offer wording, price presentation and inconsistent follow-up.

Your offer should answer five questions

  • What exactly are you selling?
  • Who is it for?
  • What does the customer receive?
  • What does it cost?
  • What should the customer do next?

Build the conversation

  • Opening message: identify the need.
  • Qualification: ask only what affects fit, price or timing.
  • Offer: make scope and price easy to scan.
  • Follow-up: one useful reminder, not repeated spam.
  • Close: confirm the next action and fulfillment expectations.

Do not confuse messaging volume with sales quality.

Current Saudi WhatsApp Business guidance emphasizes catalogue structure, quick replies, labels and respectful opt-in communication. A clearer system can reduce lost conversations without buying advertising.
