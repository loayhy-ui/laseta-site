Apply to one real vacancy with a CV built around that job.

The LASETA 48-Hour Job Application Sprint is for job seekers who already know the vacancy they want. We review the job requirements, map them against your real experience, tailor the CV and prepare the application and interview notes around that specific role.

What you receive

  • A CV tailored to the language and requirements of the target vacancy.
  • A requirements-to-experience mapping that shows where your background aligns and where gaps remain.
  • A concise application or cover-letter message for the role.
  • An interview-preparation sheet focused on the vacancy and your actual experience.

Who this is for

This is best for applicants who have a specific role in mind rather than people asking for a generic CV redesign.

  • Graduates applying to a named opening.
  • Professionals changing companies or sectors.
  • Applicants targeting roles in Riyadh, Jeddah, Dammam, Khobar or elsewhere in Saudi Arabia.
  • People who want their existing experience translated into clearer role-relevant evidence.

Job application support FAQ

How much does it cost?

SAR 350 for one accepted vacancy-specific request.

Do I pay when I submit the form?

No. Submit the vacancy first. If LASETA accepts the request, the bank-transfer instructions are sent to you privately.

Is this a generic CV-writing service?

No. The starting point is one real vacancy. The goal is to make your application more relevant to that specific role.

Will you add skills I do not have?

No. We do not create false qualifications, experience, certifications or achievements.

Do you guarantee an interview?

No. Employer screening and hiring decisions remain outside LASETA's control.

When does the 48-hour period begin?

After the request is accepted, payment is confirmed, and the complete information required for the work has been received.
