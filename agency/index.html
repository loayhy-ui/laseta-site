What to include in your request

  • Business objective and target audience.
  • Service required and expected deliverables.
  • City, venue and preferred dates.
  • Approximate quantities or guest count.
  • Budget range, if available.
  • Reference images, specifications or an existing brief.

Clear scope, no invented guarantees

Campaign and event outcomes depend on the brief, audience, timing, approvals and execution conditions. The proposal should define deliverables and responsibilities; results are not guaranteed unless a specific measurable commitment is expressly written into the agreement.

Enquiries are routed through LASETA. The contracting entity and final project scope are identified in the written proposal before any commitment.
