Admissions support FAQ

Do you guarantee admission?

No. Universities and scholarship providers make their own decisions.

Do you submit applications for the student?

No. We organize and prepare the process; official submissions and declarations remain with the applicant or authorized guardian.

Can parents use the service for a minor?

Yes. For applicants under 18, the parent or guardian is included in the intake and privacy process.
