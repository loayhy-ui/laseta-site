The first step is a short, high-level description of your current sales process. Do not upload customer lists, personal data or confidential records.

01 · Locate the stall

Identify where enquiries tend to stop moving: first response, qualification, quotation, follow-up or repeat business.

02 · Prioritize the opportunity

Consider which gaps are worth addressing first, using approximate lead volume and your description—not customer-level files.
