Clinics • Enquiry & Recall Operations

Turn clinic enquiries and recalls into a consistent operating workflow.

LASETA structures how enquiries are captured, followed up, scheduled and reported so teams can reduce missed responses and forgotten recall opportunities.

Request a Clinic Workflow Review

Typical workflow

  • Enquiry capture and categorization.
  • Appointment follow-up and reminders.
  • Recall lists and reactivation.
  • Missed-enquiry review.
  • Simple operational reporting.

Safeguards

  • No medical advice is generated by the workflow.
  • Sensitive information should be minimized to what the clinic genuinely needs.
  • The workflow supports staff operations; clinical decisions remain with qualified professionals.
