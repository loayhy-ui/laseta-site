Details that speed up a quote

  • Plant name or reference image.
  • Required height, pot size or specification.
  • Quantity and whether the request is one-time or recurring.
  • Project location and access restrictions.
  • Required delivery or collection window.
  • Any acceptable alternatives or budget range.

Availability is confirmed, not assumed

Stock, plant condition, specification, transport, lead time and pricing can vary. The written quotation must confirm the actual supply terms before an order is placed.

Enquiries are routed through LASETA. The contracting entity and final supply scope are identified in the written quotation before any commitment.
