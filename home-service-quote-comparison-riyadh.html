Home-service comparison FAQ

Are you the cleaning or moving company?

No. LASETA is the buyer-side comparison and coordination layer.

Can you guarantee the lowest price?

No. The objective is a clearer documented comparison, not a promise that any quote is the absolute lowest available in the market.

Do you hold the supplier's money?

The initial model is designed to avoid unnecessary custody of third-party supplier funds. Any commercial arrangement is confirmed before a transaction proceeds.
