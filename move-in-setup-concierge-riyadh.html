Move-in setup FAQ

What does the service cost?

The launch price is SAR 499 for one accepted move-in brief. Purchases and third-party provider charges are separate.

Do you buy inventory before I commit?

No. This service is designed to avoid speculative inventory. We source against a real customer requirement.

Can you help with only one room?

Yes. The scope can be a specific room, a set of appliances or a broader home setup depending on the request.

Do I still approve the purchases?

Yes. The final buying decision remains with you.
