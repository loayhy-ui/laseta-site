How the estimate works

  1. Calculate the difference between the current and target conversion rates.
  2. Apply your assumed recoverable share to that gap and your monthly qualified lead volume.
  3. Multiply the scenario's additional wins by average revenue per sale.
  4. Apply gross margin to estimate gross profit before overhead, tax and collection risk.
This is a planning aid, not a forecast, valuation, or guarantee. It does not prove that any revenue can be recovered. Validate assumptions against actual sales and collected cash, avoid double-counting opportunities, and do not upload customer-level or confidential data.

The calculator runs locally in your browser. It has no login, analytics submission, file upload or external calculation service.
