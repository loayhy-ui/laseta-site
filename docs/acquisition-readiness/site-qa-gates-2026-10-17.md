# LASETA Site QA — October 2026

This checklist is for internal, non-production review only. It does not imply a financial valuation or ownership transfer.

- Verify that each sitemap URL returns 200 and has one canonical URL.
- Verify each conversion page has a usable contact path and a fallback when the visitor has no local email application.
- Verify form analytics separately from accepted orders and cleared bank receipts.
- Verify repository, domain, trademarks, and source-code assignment before treating an asset as transferable company property.
- Keep agency and nursery projects outside LASETA's asset valuation unless entity-level rights are documented.
- Do not publish changes or execute commercial commitments without required approval.
