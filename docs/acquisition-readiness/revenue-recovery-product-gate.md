# LASETA Revenue Recovery Estimator — Product Gate
Date: 2026-10-16

Status: Candidate asset only; not published, sold, or booked as enterprise value.

## Product definition
A bilingual Arabic/English, standalone, privacy-first calculator for dormant B2B sales opportunities. Users supply opportunity count, average deal value, estimated response and close rates, contribution margin, and follow-up cost. It calculates illustrative deals, revenue, contribution, and net contribution.

## Acceptance criteria
- No personal or customer records requested or stored.
- No server, paid API, cookie, analytics, or external dependency.
- Validate all numeric inputs and refuse out-of-range or nonfinite values.
- Explicitly distinguish modeled opportunities from collected cash and independently verified value.
- Link any eventual CTA to existing LASETA request intake with campaign attribution.
- Independent reviewer tests the final release artifact before publication.
- No publication or material production change without the required approval.

## Evidence status
