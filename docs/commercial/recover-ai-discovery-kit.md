# LASETA Recover AI — B2B Discovery and Conversion Kit
Version: 2026-10-16 · Status: Internal draft, nonbinding

## Ideal buyer
Saudi B2B organizations with a repeatable pipeline of inquiries, quotations, follow-ups or lapsed accounts: contractors, professional services, clinics, real-estate teams and ecommerce operators. A buyer is qualified only when there is an authorized decision-maker, an existing process to inspect lawfully, a measurable commercial problem and a realistic payment route.

## Discovery questions (15 minutes)
1. How many qualified inquiries and quotations were not followed up in the last 90 days?
2. Which stages have measurable drop-off, and what is the evidence source?
3. What is the average deal value, contribution margin and historic close rate?
4. Which tools and personnel currently own follow-up?
5. Can the customer provide an anonymized aggregate export, without personal data, for an initial estimate?
6. Who authorizes scope, data processing, purchase orders and payment?
7. What outcome would justify a small fixed-scope pilot?
8. Which constraints could block execution (consent, retention, sector rules, system access)?

## Qualification score (0–10; do not fabricate)
- Measurable backlog (0–2): 0 unknown, 1 estimated, 2 documented.
- Economic upside (0–2): 0 unknown, 1 modeled, 2 backed by historical outcomes.
- Decision access (0–2): 0 none, 1 influencer, 2 authorized buyer.
- Data permission (0–2): 0 absent, 1 being clarified, 2 documented permitted scope.
- Procurement readiness (0–2): 0 unknown, 1 budget/process identified, 2 purchasing and payment path confirmed.

Priority A: 8–10; B: 5–7; C: 0–4. A high score does not establish a contract or revenue.

## Draft no-commitment offer
- Step 1: Free diagnostic conversation; no client data transfer required.
- Step 2: Provide a written, nonbinding gap map and indicative recovery model based only on client-supplied aggregates.
- Step 3: Propose a bounded pilot specifying deliverables, exclusions, acceptance tests, data permissions, price and payment terms. No execution or commitment until human approval and valid agreement.
- Step 4: Measure pilot outcome against a baseline, with customer verification. Report booked cash only after bank settlement is evidenced.

## Measurement definitions
- Lead: inbound contact with timestamp and channel, deduplicated by lawful internal identifier.
- Qualified lead: buyer and measurable problem verified; score recorded.
- Proposal: dated written nonbinding scope and price.
- Contract: signed by authorized parties; separate from invoice.
- Invoice: issued under valid tax/commercial requirements; separate from payment.
- Cash collected: bank-confirmed receipt net of reversals; never equate to inquiry, quote, invoice or verbal approval.
- MRR: contracted and active recurring revenue; distinguish contracted from collected and exclude one-time fees.

## Non-negotiable guardrails
No unsolicited messaging, scraping personal contact details, impersonation, access to client systems, transfer of customer records, or publication of case studies without documented authority. No promises of guaranteed recovered revenue. Use synthetic or anonymized aggregates for pre-sale estimates. No use of Diamond Hand agency/nursery as LASETA-owned assets without verified rights.

## Single-owner handoff
Commercial owner owns discovery, scoring, nonbinding proposals and cash-conversion follow-up.
Technical owner owns the proposed estimator, privacy review inputs and release candidate.
Opportunity owner owns sector hypotheses and lawful market research; does not contact prospects.
Finance owner owns definitions and source-of-truth checks for cash, invoices and recurring revenue.
Governance owner independently reviews evidence, rights and approval gates, and does not execute any of the other roles' work.

## Evidence and next action
Each opportunity needs a dated source, named internal role-owner, next-action due date, decision gate and verifiable outcome. Unverified entries remain hypotheses. Store client-specific records only in an authorized private system; this public repository contains no lead personal data.
