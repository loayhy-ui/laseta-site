# LASETA — Contractor Revenue Review (internal non-binding offer, v1)
Date: 2026-10-17 | Owner: Commercial | Status: DRAFT / NOT PUBLISHED / NOT QUOTED

## Buyer and outcome
Saudi contractors, project suppliers, fit-out and maintenance firms with dormant quotations, delayed commercial follow-ups, or unresolved handoffs. The service identifies **potential** revenue opportunities; it does not guarantee recovery or collections.

## Five-business-day diagnostic (subject to separately approved scope)
1. Intake: company sector, opportunity count, process owner, sales cycle, age bands and anonymized sample.
2. Classify: dormant quotes, missed follow-ups, abandoned leads, renewal/reorder signals, and disputed vs undisputed items.
3. Prioritize: owner, next permitted action, expected effort, evidence source, confidence and dependency.
4. Deliver: anonymized opportunity register, 10-action priority queue, three optional follow-up message drafts, and a 30-day measurement plan.
5. Baseline: opportunity count and value are *potential*, not revenue. Only independently verified receipts enter collected cash.

## Data and execution guardrails
- Never ask for customer IDs, account numbers, banking details, passwords or confidential contracts through the public intake form.
- Obtain lawful authorization and a data-sharing agreement before examining identifiable customer records.
- No unsolicited outbound messages, client contact, contract signature, payment, deployment or collection action without explicit authorization.
- No legal, tax, debt-collection or accounting assurance; refer disputes to licensed professionals.
- No invented testimonials, ROI, past clients, guarantees, or published price.
